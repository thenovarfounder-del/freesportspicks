Frequently Asked Questions

What is the most important factor in a sports pick?

Line value. A good pick on a bad line loses money over time. Always compare lines across multiple books before placing any bet.

How do sharp bettors differ from recreational bettors?

Sharps bet for value not action. They are selective, patient, and track every result.

What does ATS mean in sports betting?

ATS means Against The Spread — tracking how often picks cover the point spread.

Is sports betting legal?

Sports betting is legal in 38+ US states as of 2025. We only recommend betting through licensed legal sportsbooks.

What bankroll management strategy do you recommend?

Flat betting 1-3% of your total bankroll per game is standard. Never chase losses by increasing bet size.
