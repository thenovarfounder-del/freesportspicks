What Makes a Good Sports Pick

A good sports pick is about finding value. Identifying situations where the odds offered by the sportsbook are higher than the true probability of the outcome. This is called positive expected value betting and it is the only mathematically sustainable betting strategy that exists.

Line Shopping Rule: Never place a bet without checking at least 3 sportsbooks. Getting -3 instead of -3.5 or +115 instead of +105 adds up to thousands of dollars over a full season. Line shopping is free money that most recreational bettors leave on the table every week.

Sport by Sport Betting Guide

NFL Betting

Line movement is king in NFL betting. Situational edges like divisional records, rest advantages, and travel are highly predictive. Weather matters significantly for totals. The key numbers of 3 and 7 are critical - always try to get the better side of those numbers when possible.

NBA Betting

Rest and travel are the biggest edges in NBA betting. Back-to-back games, coast-to-coast travel, and load management create systematic inefficiencies the market does not always fully price. Pace matchups drive total bet value when two fast-paced teams meet.

MLB Betting

Starting pitching drives 80 percent of outcomes in baseball. Park factors, handedness splits, and bullpen usage complete the picture. Moneyline value is found by identifying overvalued aces and undervalued underdogs with strong starting pitchers.

College Football Betting

The college football betting market is significantly less efficient than the NFL. The biggest edges are in mid-major conferences where public attention is minimal and specific program knowledge provides a genuine information advantage.

Bankroll Management

  • Set a total bankroll - Money you are fully comfortable losing. Never bet with money you need for expenses.
  • Flat bet 1-3 percent per game - Standard play is 1-2 units. Reserve 3 units for your highest confidence plays only.
  • Never chase losses - Doubling bet size after a loss is the fastest path to going broke.
  • Track everything - Record every bet, every line, every result. Without data you cannot improve.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the most important factor in a sports pick?

Line value. A well-reasoned pick on a bad line loses money over time. Always compare lines across multiple books before placing any bet.

How do sharp bettors differ from recreational bettors?

Sharps bet for value not action. They are selective, patient, and track every single result. Recreational bettors bet based on team preference or narrative.

What does ATS mean?

ATS means Against The Spread. It tracks how often a team or bettor covers the point spread as opposed to just winning the game outright.

Is sports betting legal?

Sports betting is legal in 38 or more US states as of 2025. We only recommend betting through licensed legal sportsbooks in states where it is permitted.

What bankroll management do you recommend?

Flat betting 1-3 percent of your total bankroll per game is the industry standard. Never chase losses by increasing bet size.
