Frequently Asked Questions

When is NFL Week 1 2025?

The 2025 NFL season is scheduled to kick off in early September 2025. The exact Thursday Night Football opener will be announced by the NFL in May.

What is the best Week 1 NFL betting strategy?

Fade public money on national favorites, back divisional underdogs, and lean under on high totals.

Should I bet NFL in Week 1 or wait for more data?

Both approaches have merit. We recommend smaller bet sizes in Week 1 to account for higher variance.

Which NFL teams historically cover in Week 1?

Divisional underdogs, teams with experienced quarterbacks, and teams facing opponents with new offensive systems all have historical Week 1 ATS edges.

How early do NFL Week 1 lines come out?

Game-specific lines for Week 1 typically open in the week leading up to the first game.
