NFL Point Spreads Explained

The point spread levels the playing field between any two teams. Standard NFL spread bets are priced at -110 on both sides. To break even you need to win 52.4 percent of your bets. To profit consistently you need to win 55 percent or better. Our free NFL picks aim to run at 56-60 percent over a full season.

NFL Totals Strategy

Wind over 15 mph kills passing games and suppresses scoring. Cold weather under 32 degrees reduces average scoring by roughly 2 points. Dome games average 3-4 more points than outdoor stadiums. When these factors exist and the total does not reflect them you have value on the under.
