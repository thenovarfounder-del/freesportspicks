NFL Point Spreads Explained

The point spread is the foundation of NFL betting. It exists to level the playing field between mismatched opponents and to attract equal action on both sides of a game. The sportsbook goal is not to predict the outcome but to split the money evenly so they earn the vig regardless of who wins.

This is critical to understand because it means NFL lines are not always accurate reflections of true team strength. They reflect public perception. When public perception is wrong and a popular team is overvalued there is a betting edge. Finding those edges every week is exactly what our handicappers do.

Standard NFL spread bets are priced at -110 on both sides. You bet 110 dollars to win 100. To break even you need to win 52.4 percent of your bets. To profit consistently you need to win 55 percent or better. Our free NFL picks aim to run at 56-60 percent over a full season.

NFL Totals Betting Strategy

NFL totals betting is one of the most exploitable markets in sports betting. The public overwhelmingly bets overs because people want to see scoring and touchdowns. This public bias toward overs creates systematic value on unders in specific situations that sharp bettors identify every single week.

Wind over 15 mph kills passing games and suppresses scoring significantly. Cold weather under 32 degrees reduces average NFL scoring by roughly 2 points. Dome games average 3-4 more points than outdoor stadiums. When a game with wind and cold has a high total set by the sportsbook you have a clear value bet on the under.

The best under situations we track every week: cold weather outdoor games below 32 degrees, games with sustained wind over 20 mph, games featuring two top-10 defenses, and primetime rivalry games where defensive intensity dominates. In these four situations combined the under hits at above-market rates consistently over the last five NFL seasons.

NFL Parlay Betting Guide

Parlays combine multiple picks into one bet. Win all legs and the payout multiplies dramatically. Miss one leg and you lose everything. The sportsbook edge on parlays is significantly higher than individual bets and compounds with every leg you add.

  • 2-team parlay - True odds 3-1, books pay 2.6-1. House edge roughly 10 percent
  • 3-team parlay - True odds 7-1, books pay 6-1. House edge roughly 12 percent
  • 4-team parlay - True odds 15-1, books pay 12-1. House edge roughly 20 percent
  • 5-team parlay - True odds 31-1, books pay 25-1. House edge roughly 19 percent

We recommend limiting parlays to 2 teams maximum. Flat betting single games beats parlays over any significant sample size. The excitement of parlay payouts comes at a serious mathematical cost that compounds over a full NFL season.
