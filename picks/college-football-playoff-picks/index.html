Betting the Expanded 12-Team Playoff

The expanded playoff created an entirely new betting landscape. First-round games are played on campus, giving higher seeds a genuine home-field edge that the market is still learning to price. The additional rounds mean teams face layoffs, travel, and physical tolls that factor heavily into handicapping. Byes for top seeds, rematch dynamics, and the motivation gap between a team thrilled just to make the field and one with title expectations all create value across the bracket.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the expanded College Football Playoff work?

The expanded College Football Playoff features a 12-team format with first-round games played on the campus of higher seeds, byes for the top seeds, and additional rounds leading to the national championship. This creates more matchups and betting opportunities.

Does home field matter in the College Football Playoff?

Yes. First-round playoff games are played on the campus of the higher seed, giving them a genuine home-field advantage that the market is still learning to price. This campus edge is an important handicapping angle in the expanded format.

How do you handicap College Football Playoff games?

We weigh the campus home-field edge in first-round games, rest and layoff differentials between rounds, style matchups, and how each team's strengths translate against a specific opponent, while also tracking championship futures value.
