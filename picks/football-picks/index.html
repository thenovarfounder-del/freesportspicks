Frequently Asked Questions

Is college football or NFL easier to bet?

College football has more inefficient markets especially in smaller conferences. NFL is more consistent for disciplined bettors.

What is the key line in football betting?

The key numbers in football are 3 and 7. Avoid laying -3.5 or +3.5 if you can get better positioning.

When does college football season start?

The college football season typically starts in late August or early September.

What does covering the spread mean in football?

Covering means the team you bet won by more than the spread as a favorite or lost by less than the spread as an underdog.

How do weather conditions affect football picks?

Wind over 15 mph dramatically reduces passing and scoring. We always check weather before finalizing picks for outdoor stadiums.
