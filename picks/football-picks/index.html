How do you pick NFL games against the spread?

What is a point spread in football?

A point spread is a handicap assigned to each team to level the betting field. If the Chiefs are -3.5, they must win by 4 or more for a spread bet to win. If you bet the underdog +3.5, they can lose by up to 3 and still cover.

NFL vs College Football Betting

Market Efficiency: The NFL is the sharpest betting market in sports. College football is far less efficient especially outside the Power Five conferences where public attention and sharp money are minimal.

Spread Size: College football spreads are routinely 20, 30, or even 40 points when elite programs face weaker opponents. NFL spreads almost never exceed 14 points.

Home Field Advantage: Home field is worth roughly 2.5 to 3 points in the NFL. In college football it can be worth 5 to 8 points at venues like Baton Rouge, Columbus, Tuscaloosa, and Ann Arbor.

Injury Information: NFL injury reports are mandated by the league with standardized designations. College injury information is largely informal with coaches routinely giving vague or misleading status updates. This creates real edges for prepared researchers.

College Football by Conference

SEC Betting

The SEC is the most watched and most bet college football conference. Alabama, Georgia, LSU, and Florida attract enormous public money that distorts lines regularly. Defensive football dominates the conference and SEC totals go under at a higher rate than any other Power Five conference.

Big Ten Betting

Ohio State, Michigan, and Penn State drive Big Ten betting volume. Big Ten games feature strong home field advantages particularly in Columbus, Ann Arbor, and Happy Valley. Conference parity has increased significantly over the last three seasons.

MAC and Sun Belt Betting

This is where real money is made in college football betting. Books cannot perfectly price 200-plus games per week and these conferences get the least sharp attention. Team-specific knowledge of programs like Toledo, Western Kentucky, and Appalachian State creates genuine edges that do not exist in high-attention markets.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is college football or NFL easier to bet profitably?

College football has more inefficient markets especially in smaller conferences. But larger spreads and less public information make it higher variance. The NFL is more consistent for disciplined bettors with a strong process.

What is the key line in football betting?

The most important key numbers are 3 and 7 in the NFL. These margins appear in roughly 15-20 percent of all NFL games. Avoid laying -3.5 or taking +2.5 if you can get a better number at any book.

When does college football season start?

The college football season typically begins in late August or early September. The CFP National Championship is played in January.

What does covering the spread mean?

Covering means the team you bet either won by more than the spread as a favorite or lost by less than the spread or won outright as an underdog.

How does weather affect football picks?

Wind over 15 mph dramatically reduces passing and scoring. Rain affects ball handling and field goal accuracy. Always check weather before betting outdoor football games from October through January.
