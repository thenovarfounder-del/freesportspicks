NBA Props Strategy

NBA props are the most liquid prop market in sports betting. The key edges are pace matchups — a fast-paced team facing a slow defense creates over value on scoring props — and defensive schemes that limit specific positions.

  • Track pace of play (possessions per 48)
  • Identify defensive mismatches
  • Monitor minutes and rotation changes
  • Back-to-back games affect player output significantly
