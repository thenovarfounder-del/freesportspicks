How NFL Teasers Work

In a six-point teaser, you select two or more teams and shift each spread six points in your favor. For example, a team favored by 7.5 becomes favored by just 1.5, and an underdog getting 2.5 now gets 8.5. All teased legs must win for the teaser to pay, similar to a parlay but with the added cushion of the adjusted line. The payout is lower than a standard parlay because you are buying those extra points, but the improved probability often makes teasers a strong value.

The Key to Teaser Strategy: Key Numbers

The most important concept in NFL teaser betting is crossing the key numbers of 3 and 7, the two most common margins of victory in the NFL. The classic profitable teaser, often called the Wong teaser, involves teasing favorites of 7.5 to 8.5 down through both 7 and 3, or teasing underdogs of 1.5 to 2.5 up through 3 and 7. Moving through both key numbers dramatically improves your odds because so many NFL games land on those exact margins. This is why disciplined teaser bettors focus on specific spread ranges rather than teasing any game.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an NFL teaser?

An NFL teaser is a bet that lets you adjust the point spread in your favor across two or more games in exchange for a lower payout. The standard NFL teaser moves each line six points. All teased legs must win for the bet to pay out.

What is a Wong teaser?

A Wong teaser is a six-point teaser strategy that teases favorites of 7.5 to 8.5 down through both key numbers of 7 and 3, or underdogs of 1.5 to 2.5 up through 3 and 7. Crossing both key numbers dramatically improves win probability.

Why are the numbers 3 and 7 important in teasers?

3 and 7 are the two most common margins of victory in the NFL because of the scoring value of field goals and touchdowns. Teasers that move a spread through both numbers gain the most value, since so many games land on those exact margins.

How many teams should I tease?

Two-team six-point teasers through the key numbers offer the best mathematical value. Larger multi-team teasers lower your payout and win probability, eroding the edge. Disciplined teaser bettors focus on two-team tickets in optimal spread ranges.
