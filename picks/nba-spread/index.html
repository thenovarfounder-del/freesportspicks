NBA Spread Betting Explained

NBA spread betting means picking a team to win or lose by a certain number of points. If the Lakers are -6.5, they must win by 7 or more for a spread bet on them to win. Spread betting equalizes lopsided matchups and gives you a way to bet on heavy favorites at better value than the moneyline.

NBA Spread Strategy

  • Home court advantage: Worth 3-4 points in the NBA. Teams at home cover spreads at a higher rate.
  • Back-to-back spots: Teams on the second night of a back-to-back underperform against the spread
  • Blowout avoidance: NBA teams often pull starters in blowouts — avoid laying large spreads
  • Pace mismatches: Fast teams vs slow teams create predictable scoring patterns

How Our AI Picks NBA Spreads

Our model calculates expected margin of victory for every NBA game using offensive and defensive efficiency ratings, pace, rest days, and travel schedules. When our projected margin differs significantly from the posted spread, we flag it as a value bet.
