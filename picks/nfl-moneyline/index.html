NFL Moneyline Betting Explained

NFL moneyline betting means picking the straight-up winner with no point spread involved. A favorite is listed with a minus sign (e.g. -180) and an underdog with a plus sign (e.g. +155). The moneyline is best used when you love an underdog or want to avoid the spread on a big favorite.

When to Bet NFL Moneylines

  • Home underdogs: Best moneyline value in the NFL. Home dogs cover and win outright more than the market prices in.
  • Divisional games: Tighter games where spreads are riskier
  • Weather games: Low-scoring games favor the running team — moneyline on the stronger run game
  • Must-win spots: Desperate teams play harder

NFL Moneyline Strategy

The key to NFL moneyline profitability is finding spots where the implied probability in the line doesn't match your actual win probability estimate. Our AI calculates win probability for every game and flags the biggest discrepancies as value bets.
