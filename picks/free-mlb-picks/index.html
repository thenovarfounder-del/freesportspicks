Free MLB Picks FAQ

How do MLB run lines work?

The MLB run line is baseball's point spread, typically set at 1.5 runs. Betting the favorite -1.5 means they must win by 2 or more; the underdog +1.5 can lose by 1 and still cover.

Are weather conditions important for MLB picks?

Absolutely. Wind speed and direction at outdoor stadiums significantly affects over/under totals. Wind blowing out at Wrigley Field historically inflates scoring.

What makes a good MLB moneyline pick?

Pitching matchup advantage is the biggest factor. Starting pitcher ERA, opposing lineup's batting average vs. hand, and bullpen strength drive our moneyline model.
