Prop Bet Picks FAQ

What does 'ATS' mean in sports betting?

'Against the Spread' (ATS) refers to betting on the point spread rather than the moneyline. A team can win the game outright but still fail to cover the spread if they don't win by enough.

What is a parlay bet?

A parlay combines two or more individual bets into one wager. All legs must win for the parlay to pay out. The payout increases with each added leg, but risk increases significantly.

How do I read betting odds?

American odds show how much you win on a $100 bet. -110 means you bet $110 to win $100. +150 means you win $150 on a $100 bet. Negative odds = favorite; positive odds = underdog.
