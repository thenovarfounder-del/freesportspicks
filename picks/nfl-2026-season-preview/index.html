NFL 2026 Season Preview: Predictions, Odds & Best Bets

Your complete guide to the 2026 NFL season. Super Bowl LXI contenders, division races, win totals, and where to find free picks all season long.

The 2026 Super Bowl Picture

The road to Super Bowl LXI runs through a wide-open field. The defending champion Seattle Seahawks stunned the league last season, beating the New England Patriots 29-13 in Super Bowl LX. But the offseason reshuffled the contenders in a big way.

Los Angeles Rams — Super Bowl Favorites

The Rams headline the 2026 contenders after a blockbuster trade for reigning Defensive Player of the Year Myles Garrett, moving them to roughly +550 to +600 Super Bowl odds. With Matthew Stafford confirming his return and a loaded receiving corps, Los Angeles is built to win now.

Seattle Seahawks — Defending Champions

Seattle rides its championship momentum into 2026 as a top contender, anchored by an elite defense. The Seahawks headline a brutally deep NFC West and open the season in a Super Bowl rematch with New England.

Buffalo Bills — AFC Frontrunner

The Bills sit atop the AFC Championship odds, with Josh Allen still chasing his first Super Bowl ring. Buffalo remains a heavy favorite to win the AFC East once again.

Baltimore Ravens — Rising Contender

With Lamar Jackson and Derrick Henry powering the backfield and a new-look staff, the Ravens are expected to return to serious playoff contention in the AFC North.

Kansas City Chiefs — The Wild Card

The Chiefs enter 2026 at around +1500 as Patrick Mahomes returns from a late-season knee injury. A retooled roster means Kansas City has less margin for error, but no one counts out Mahomes in January.

Division Races to Watch

The NFC West headlines the 2026 divisional landscape as arguably the deepest division in football, with the Rams, Seahawks, and 49ers all ranking among many preseason top-five teams. The AFC East runs through Buffalo, while the NFC North sets up as a tight battle between the Lions and Packers. In the AFC North, the Ravens aim to reclaim the crown behind Lamar Jackson.

Division winners earn automatic playoff berths, and the top seed in each conference gets a first-round bye, making these races critical for postseason positioning.
