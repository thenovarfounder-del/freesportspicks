The Expanded 48-Team Format

The 2026 World Cup introduces a dramatically new structure. With 48 teams instead of 32, the tournament features an expanded group stage followed by a larger knockout bracket, meaning more matches, more upsets, and far more betting opportunities than any previous World Cup. The expanded field brings in nations that rarely reach the tournament, creating value in matchups where the market has limited data on unfamiliar teams. Understanding how the new format shapes advancement and motivation is central to betting it well.

How to Bet the World Cup

World Cup betting spans several markets. Match betting includes the three-way moneyline of home, draw, or away, since soccer matches can end level. Totals center on goals, most commonly the over/under 2.5 goals line. Futures markets let you back a team to win the group, reach the final, or lift the trophy, and these offer strong value early before the field narrows. Frequently Asked Questions

When and where is the 2026 World Cup?

The 2026 FIFA World Cup is co-hosted across three nations, the United States, Canada, and Mexico. It is the first World Cup to expand to 48 teams, making it the largest in the tournament's history with more matches than ever before.

How does the 48-team World Cup format work?

The expanded 2026 World Cup features 48 teams instead of 32, with an enlarged group stage followed by a larger knockout bracket. This means more matches, more potential upsets, and significantly more betting opportunities than previous tournaments.

How do you bet on the World Cup?

World Cup betting includes three-way match moneylines (home, draw, or away), goal totals like over/under 2.5, and futures such as group winner, reaching the final, or winning the trophy. Futures offer strong value early before the field narrows.

Do host nations have an advantage in the World Cup?

Historically, host nations tend to overperform due to home support, familiar conditions, and reduced travel. With the US, Canada, and Mexico co-hosting in 2026, home-nation advantage is a betting angle worth watching throughout the tournament.
