Understanding NFL Point Spreads

The point spread exists to level the playing field and attract equal action on both sides. NFL lines reflect public perception not always true team strength. When public perception is wrong and a popular team is overvalued there is a betting edge.

Key Concept: A team can be better than its opponent and still not cover the spread. The spread is about margin not about who wins. A 3-point favorite that wins by 2 is a loss against the spread even though they won the game outright.

Frequently Asked Questions

What NFL bets have the best value?

Single-game point spreads and totals at standard -110 juice offer the best value. The house edge is lowest on these bets compared to parlays and teasers.

How do NFL spreads work?

The spread gives points to the underdog and takes points from the favorite. A -6.5 favorite must win by 7 or more to cover. A +6.5 underdog must lose by 6 or fewer or win outright to cover.

What time do NFL lines open?

Lines for the following week typically open Sunday night or Monday morning after that week's games finish.
