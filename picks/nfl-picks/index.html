Frequently Asked Questions

What NFL bets have the best value?

Single-game point spreads and totals offer the best value. The house edge is lowest at standard -110 juice. Avoid heavy parlays.

How do NFL spreads work?

The spread gives points to the underdog and takes points from the favorite. A -6.5 favorite must win by 7+ to cover.

What time do NFL lines open?

Lines for the following week typically open Sunday night or Monday morning after games finish.
