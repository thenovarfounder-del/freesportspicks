2025 NFL Division Predictions

AFC East - Buffalo Bills

Josh Allen entering his prime with a fully balanced roster is the best combination in the AFC East. New England is rebuilding, Miami has quarterback injury concerns, and the Jets are still developing their offensive infrastructure.

AFC North - Baltimore Ravens

Lamar Jackson is the best player in the AFC. His 15-5 ATS record in divisional games speaks to his ability to dominate within the conference. Baltimore has the most complete roster in the AFC North.

AFC South - Houston Texans

CJ Stroud has developed into a genuine franchise quarterback. The Texans have the best roster in the division and the coaching staff to compete for an AFC Championship appearance in 2025.

AFC West - Kansas City Chiefs

Until proven otherwise this is Andy Reid and Patrick Mahomes division. Denver is improving and represents the most credible challenge but the Chiefs playoff experience edges them out again in 2025.

NFC East - Philadelphia Eagles

Jalen Hurts and a defensive line that terrorizes quarterbacks make the Eagles the clear class of the NFC East. Dallas will be competitive but Philadelphia has better roster construction from top to bottom.

NFC North - Detroit Lions

The Lions 2024 momentum carries directly into 2025. Detroit has the most explosive offense in the NFC North and a coaching staff that has proven it can win the most important games.

NFC South - Tampa Bay Buccaneers

Baker Mayfield's resurgence in Tampa Bay continues into 2025. The Buccaneers have the best coaching staff in the division. Atlanta and New Orleans are competitive but Tampa Bay edges them out.

NFC West - San Francisco 49ers

When healthy the 49ers are the best team in the NFC. Kyle Shanahan's offensive system is unmatched in the league and their defensive personnel is elite. Health is the only question for San Francisco in 2025.
