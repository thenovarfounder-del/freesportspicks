The Truth About the Pick Selling Industry

Guaranteed Winners Do Not Exist: No pick in sports betting is ever guaranteed. Anyone advertising guaranteed locks is lying. The best handicappers in the world lose 40 percent or more of their picks.

Cherry-Picked Records: Many paid services publish only their winning picks and quietly bury losses. Our records are published chronologically and completely with every result included win or lose.

The Package Split Scam: Some services send Team A to half their subscribers and Team B to the other half. One group always gets the winner. Then both groups get an email saying they received the winning pick and encouraging renewal. This is widespread fraud in the industry.
