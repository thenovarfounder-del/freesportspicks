Never risk more than 1-5% of your total bankroll on any single game, regardless of confidence level. If you're starting with a $500 bankroll, your typical bet should range from $5-25 depending on confidence.

Track Your Results

Maintain a detailed betting journal documenting every wager you place based on our picks. Record the game, pick type, odds, stake amount, and result. This tracking helps you identify which bet types are most profitable for your approach and provides accountability for your betting decisions.

Shop for the Best Lines

Having accounts at multiple sportsbooks allows you to compare odds and secure the best available lines. Getting -6.5 instead of -7 or +105 instead of -110 might seem insignificant on individual bets, but these small edges compound significantly over hundreds of wagers throughout a season.

While parlays offer attractive payouts, they significantly reduce your expected value and increase variance. Focus on straight bets where you can leverage our expert analysis most effectively. Building your bankroll requires consistent, sustainable profits rather than chasing big parlay scores.

Specialize and Focus

College basketball offers hundreds of games throughout the season, but you don't need to bet every game. Focus on the picks where our analysis identifies the strongest value propositions. Selective betting with quality picks outperforms high-volume wagering on marginal opportunities.
