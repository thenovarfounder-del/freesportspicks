Our Commitment to Responsible Gambling

At FreeSportsPicks.pro, we believe sports betting should be entertaining, not harmful. We provide free picks to inform your betting decisions, but we also take seriously our responsibility to promote safe gambling practices.

Responsible Gambling Guidelines

  • Set a budget: Only bet what you can afford to lose. Treat it as entertainment spending, not investment.
  • Set time limits: Decide in advance how much time you will spend on betting activities.
  • Never chase losses: Accept losses as part of betting. Never bet more to try to recover losses.
  • Don't borrow to bet: Never use credit cards, loans, or money borrowed from others for betting.
  • Keep betting separate: Never use money needed for bills, rent, food, or family expenses.
  • Take breaks: Regular breaks from betting keep it in perspective as entertainment.

⚠️ Warning Signs of Problem Gambling

  • Spending more money or time on gambling than intended
  • Feeling anxious, irritable, or restless when not gambling
  • Chasing losses with bigger bets
  • Hiding gambling activity from family or friends
  • Borrowing money or selling possessions to fund gambling
  • Neglecting work, school, or family responsibilities
  • Thinking about gambling constantly

Self-Exclusion Tools

All licensed sportsbooks offer self-exclusion programs that allow you to ban yourself from gambling for a set period. Contact your sportsbook directly or use your state's self-exclusion registry.

Help Resources
