39.5 at MetLife, 49.5 at Lambeau

This board answers a question the season has been posing since October: how much of a low December total is the weather, and how much is the teams?

Denver at the Jets carries 39.5. Buffalo at Green Bay carries 49.5. Both are outdoor games in northern venues in mid-December. Ten points separate them.

The weather is not ten points different between New Jersey and Wisconsin in December. The teams are. A total prices the offences first and the forecast second, and this board makes that unusually clear.

Lambeau at 49.5

Sunday night, mid-December, one of the coldest venues in the league, and the market expects fifty points. It is a statement about two offences it rates highly enough to overcome conditions it understands perfectly well.

Green Bay lays 1.5, so it also expects the game to be close. High total, tight line — the market's picture is two good offences and two defences it does not fear.

Cleveland again

Atlanta at Cleveland carries 40.5, and Cleveland has now been involved in the lowest or joint-lowest total on four separate boards this season. Some of that is a rotating cast of visiting offences; a good deal is the venue and the calendar.

No double digits for a third week

Detroit -7.5 and Seattle -7.5 top the board. Weeks 12, 13 and 14 have now passed without a single double-digit spread — the longest such run of the season.

Four games sit at 1.5 or under: Atlanta at Cleveland, Houston at Washington, Kansas City at Cincinnati, and Buffalo at Green Bay. The market's view of the league has compressed rather than separated as the season has gone on.

Kansas City at Cincinnati at 1.5

A 48.5 total in Cincinnati in December with a line inside two points. Same combination as Lambeau — the market rates both offences and expects a close, high-scoring game in conditions that would usually suppress both.

The Rams at Levi's carries 49.5

The other 49.5 on the board, and this one indoors-adjacent in northern California where December weather is mild. That is the number you would expect for two offences the market respects, without the conditions argument attached.

Worth noting the contrast: Lambeau and Levi's carry the same total in the same week, in entirely different climates. Again, the teams are doing the work.
