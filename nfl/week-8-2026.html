39.5 at Acrisure: the lowest number of the season

Cleveland at Pittsburgh carries a total of 39.5, below anything the first seven weeks produced.

Three things are stacked into that number. It is a divisional game, where familiarity compresses scoring as well as margins. It is an outdoor stadium in early November, when Pittsburgh's conditions start to matter. And the market does not rate either offence highly.

The seasonal pattern is now visible across our weekly boards: the lowest total was 40.5 in Week 4, 39.5 here, and outdoor numbers will keep drifting down through December. A total is a forecast about weather as much as about football.

Eight divisional games out of fourteen

Cleveland at Pittsburgh, Kansas City at Denver, New England at Miami, Philadelphia at Washington, Las Vegas at the Jets, Minnesota at Detroit, Atlanta at Tampa Bay, and the Chargers at the Rams.

That is more than half the slate, and the pricing follows the pattern that has held all season: divisional games are priced tightly. Six of the eight sit at 4.5 or under, with Kansas City at Denver at 1.5 and Philadelphia at Washington at 1.5.

Kansas City at Denver at 1.5

A divisional game at altitude priced at a point and a half. Whatever the records say, the market is treating this as a coin flip — and Denver's home environment is one of the few genuine venue advantages in the league.

Chargers at Rams: the crosstown game

Both franchises share SoFi Stadium, so the Chargers travel nowhere to play a road game. The Rams are listed as home at -3.5.

It is worth thinking about what home advantage means in that circumstance. There is no travel, no unfamiliar surface, no time zone, and the visiting team practises down the road. What remains is crowd composition and the logistics of which locker room you use.

Markets typically apply a smaller home adjustment to these fixtures for exactly that reason, though it is rarely stated openly.

The top of the board

Dallas -10.5 over Arizona is the only double-digit number, and Arizona has now appeared as a heavy underdog in consecutive weeks after LAR -13.5 in Week 6.

At the other end, Baltimore at Buffalo carries 51.5, the highest total on the board, in an outdoor stadium in November. That is the market backing two offences against the conditions rather than the other way round.

Reading the numbers on this page

Line shows the favourite and the points they must win by. Total is the combined score expected from both teams, and you bet whether the result finishes above or below it.

The half points prevent ties — a team cannot win by one and a half, so -1.5 cannot push. Full mechanics: how to read betting lines.
