How Sportsbook Bonuses Work

Welcome bonuses are designed to attract new customers — but their real value varies dramatically based on wagering requirements, time limits, and eligible bet types. Understanding these details separates good offers from bad ones.

Types of Welcome Bonuses

Deposit Match Bonuses

The most common type. Deposit $500, receive $500 in bonus funds. Wagering requirements determine how many times you must bet the bonus before withdrawing. Always calculate the realistic value after requirements.

Free Bet Credits

Free bets pay winnings only — the stake is not returned. A $100 free bet that wins at -110 returns approximately $91, not $191. This makes free bets worth roughly 70 cents on the dollar compared to cash.

No-Deposit Bonuses

Rare but valuable — free credits given just for signing up without depositing. These carry strict wagering requirements but represent pure added value with no financial commitment.

Evaluating Bonus Value

True bonus value = (bonus amount) × (conversion rate). A $500 deposit match with 10x rollover requires $5,000 in bets. At -110 with a 53% win rate, you'll generate roughly $50-75 in profit from the bonus after meeting requirements.

Bonus Hunting Strategy

Open accounts at multiple books sequentially to maximize welcome offers. Use our free picks to help meet wagering requirements without deviating from your normal betting strategy.
