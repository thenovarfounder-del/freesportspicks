NFL Preseason Betting Guide 2026

FSP
FreeSportsPicks.pro Expert Team
2026-06-28 · 8 min read

NFL preseason betting requires a completely different approach than regular season betting. Understanding what drives preseason outcomes is the key to finding consistent value in August.

How NFL Preseason Differs from Regular Season

Preseason games are fundamentally different from regular season games in ways that directly impact betting outcomes. Starters play 1-2 series in early preseason games, then backup players take over for the majority of the contest. Teams are evaluating roster players and installing schemes — not trying to win.

Key NFL Preseason Betting Factors

Backup Quarterback Quality

Since starters barely play, the backup quarterback is the single most important factor in early preseason games. Research depth charts thoroughly before placing any preseason bet. A team with a capable backup QB has significant advantages over teams with weak backups.

Coaching Philosophy

Some coaches take preseason seriously — Bill Belichick historically used preseason to install systems and achieved strong results. Others rest everyone and care nothing about scores. Research each coach's preseason philosophy and historical win rate specifically for preseason games.

Position Battles

Teams with fierce competition at key positions play harder. When two quarterbacks are competing for a starting job, the team plays with extra intensity. Identify these situations before betting.

NFL Preseason Betting Strategy

  • Bet first halves: When starters are playing, games are more predictable
  • Unders have value: Backup QBs and conservative play-calling suppress scoring
  • Avoid large favorites: Preseason variance is extremely high
  • Track coaching history: Coaches with strong preseason records consistently outperform
  • Research depth charts: Know exactly who is playing before every bet

2026 NFL Preseason Schedule

The 2026 NFL preseason kicks off with the Hall of Fame Game in early August. All 32 teams play four preseason games before the regular season begins in September. Use this period to build your handicapping database before real money is on the line in the regular season.

Common Preseason Betting Mistakes

  • Treating preseason exactly like regular season betting
  • Overvaluing star players who play one quarter
  • Ignoring backup quality — the most important preseason factor
  • Betting too many games — preseason variance rewards selectivity
