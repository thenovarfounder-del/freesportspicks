NHL Puck Line Explained: Hockey Spread Betting Guide

FSP
FreeSportsPicks.pro Expert Team
2026-06-28 · 7 min read

The NHL puck line is hockey's version of the point spread. Almost every game has a puck line of -1.5 for the favorite and +1.5 for the underdog. Understanding when to use the puck line vs moneyline is essential for hockey bettors.

How the Puck Line Works

If the Bruins are -1.5 on the puck line, they must win by 2 or more goals for a puck line bet to win. The Leafs at +1.5 can lose by 1 goal and still win the bet. The puck line comes with adjusted odds — favorites pay more, underdogs pay much better.

When to Bet the Puck Line

  • Bet puck line favorites when an elite goalie faces a weak offense — blowout potential is high
  • Bet puck line underdogs when two evenly matched teams play — close game likely
  • Avoid puck line favorites in back-to-back games — tired teams play conservatively

Goaltending and the Puck Line

A hot goalie can keep any game close. Always confirm starting goalies before betting the puck line. A backup goalie for the underdog makes the puck line favorite much more attractive.
