NFL Week 1 Betting Preview 2026

FSP
FreeSportsPicks.pro Expert Team
2026-06-28 · 7 min read

NFL Week 1 2026 kicks off in September with all 32 teams in action simultaneously for the first time. Here's our complete betting preview for opening weekend.

Week 1 Betting Approach

We approach Week 1 differently than any other week. Without game film from this season, we rely heavily on preseason performance, coaching history, and roster evaluation rather than team trends.

Key Week 1 Angles for 2026

New Coaching Staffs

Teams with new head coaches in 2026 are historical fades in Week 1. System installation isn't complete, communication is developing, and execution suffers in the first real game.

Revenge Spots

Multiple teams enter 2026 facing opponents they lost to last season in meaningful games. Media creates compelling revenge narratives that inflate lines — creating value on the other side.

Home Underdogs

Week 1 home underdogs getting 3+ points have strong historical ATS performance. The crowd energy combined with undervalued home field creates consistent value in Week 1 specifically.
