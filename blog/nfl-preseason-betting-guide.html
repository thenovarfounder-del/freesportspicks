NFL Preseason Betting Guide 2026

FSP
FreeSportsPicks.pro Expert Team
June 27, 2026 · 7 min read

NFL preseason betting is often overlooked, but it can be profitable if you know what to look for. Unlike regular season games, preseason has unique characteristics that create betting opportunities for informed bettors.

How NFL Preseason Differs from Regular Season

Preseason NFL games are fundamentally different from regular season games in several key ways that affect how you should approach betting.

  • Starting Lineups: Starters play 1-2 series, then backups take over
  • Different Motivations: Teams evaluate players, not just win
  • Limited Schemes: Teams run basic plays, not full game plans
  • Inconsistent Performance: Hard to predict how teams will perform

NFL Preseason Betting Strategies

Strategy 1: Focus on Backups

Since starters barely play, focus on backup quarterbacks and skill position players. These players are fighting for roster spots and play with maximum effort — motivation matters in preseason.

Strategy 2: Track Coaching Tendencies

Some coaches take preseason more seriously than others. Bill Belichick historically used preseason to install systems. Research each coach's preseason philosophy and win rate before betting.

Strategy 3: Look for Motivated Teams

Teams with new coaching staffs or quarterbacks competing for starting jobs tend to play harder and more organized in preseason. New OCs want to install their systems quickly.

Common Preseason Betting Mistakes

  • Treating preseason like regular season — it's fundamentally different
  • Overvaluing starters who only play one quarter
  • Ignoring coaching decisions about how long starters play
  • Betting too many games — be selective

Best Preseason Bet Types

  • First Half Lines: When starters are playing — more predictable
  • Team Totals: Generally lower than regular season, unders have value
  • Backup QB Props: Less market efficiency — books don't know these guys as well
