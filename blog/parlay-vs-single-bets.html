Parlay vs Single Bets: Which Strategy is Better?

FSP
FreeSportsPicks.pro Expert Team
2026-06-28 · 7 min read

The parlay vs single bet debate is one of the most common questions in sports betting. The answer depends on your goals, bankroll size, and betting philosophy.

What is a Parlay?

A parlay combines multiple bets into one wager. All selections must win for the parlay to pay. The potential payout is much higher than single bets, but the probability of winning drops significantly with each added leg.

What is a Single Bet?

A single straight bet on one game. Lower potential payout but higher win probability. Professional bettors predominantly use single bets for this reason.

The Math of Parlays

A 3-team parlay at -110 each pays roughly 6:1. But the true probability of winning three -110 bets in a row is only about 12.5%. The sportsbook has a massive edge on parlays versus single bets.

When Parlays Make Sense

  • Small bankroll wanting higher upside
  • Two bets that are correlated (same game parlays)
  • Entertainment value with a small portion of bankroll

Professional Bettors Use Singles

Sharp bettors overwhelmingly bet single games. The edge in sports betting is small — compounding that edge across parlays is mathematically inferior to single bets over time.
