Why Line Shopping is Critical for Sports Bettors

FSP
FreeSportsPicks.pro Expert Team
2026-06-28 · 7 min read

Line shopping — comparing odds at multiple sportsbooks and always betting the best number — is the most underutilized edge available to sports bettors. Even casual bettors can improve their results dramatically by having accounts at 3-4 books.

The Value of Half a Point

In NFL betting, the most common final margins are 3 and 7 points. Getting a team at +3.5 instead of +3 can be the difference between a push and a win. Over 100 bets, a half point advantage translates to roughly 1-2 extra wins — worth thousands of dollars.

How Much Can Line Shopping Save?

A bettor making 500 bets per year who consistently finds a half point better number wins approximately 10-15 additional bets. At $100 per bet, that is $1,000-1,500 in additional profit from line shopping alone.

Best Sportsbooks for Line Shopping

Different books specialize in different sports. Sharp books like Pinnacle and Circa offer the most accurate lines. Recreational books like DraftKings and FanDuel sometimes have better numbers on popular games due to public money influence.

Reduced Juice Books

Some sportsbooks offer -105 instead of -110 on spreads. Betting -105 instead of -110 reduces the break-even win rate from 52.4% to 51.2% — a massive long-term edge for high-volume bettors.
